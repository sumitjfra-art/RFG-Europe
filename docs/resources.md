## International Standards

### IEC Standards

#### Generator Connection Standards
- **IEC 61400 series**: Wind turbine systems
  - IEC 61400-21: Power quality measurements for grid-connected wind turbines
  - IEC 61400-25: Communications for wind power plants
- **IEC 61727**: Photovoltaic (PV) systems - Characteristics of the utility interface
- **IEC 62109**: Safety of power converters for PV systems
- **IEC 61850**: Communication networks and systems for power utility automation
- **IEC 60909**: Short-circuit currents in three-phase AC systems

#### Power Quality Standards
- **IEC 61000 series**: Electromagnetic compatibility (EMC)
  - IEC 61000-2-2: Compatibility levels for LV networks
  - IEC 61000-3-6: Assessment of emission limits for distorting loads
  - IEC 61000-3-7: Assessment of emission limits for fluctuating loads
  - IEC 61000-4 series: Testing and measurement techniques

#### Protection Standards
- **IEC 60255**: Measuring relays and protection equipment
- **IEC 62351**: Power systems management and information security

### EN Standards (CENELEC)

- **EN 50549-1**: Requirements for generating plants to be connected in parallel with LV distribution networks
- **EN 50549-2**: Requirements for generating plants to be connected in parallel with MV distribution networks
- **EN 50438**: Requirements for micro-generating plants (older, being replaced)
- **EN 61000 series**: EMC standards (harmonized with IEC)

### IEEE Standards (Reference)

- **IEEE 1547**: Standard for interconnecting distributed resources with electric power systems
- **IEEE 519**: Recommended practices for harmonic control
- **IEEE C37 series**: Protection and control standards

### Purchasing Standards

- **IEC Webstore**: https://webstore.iec.ch/
- **CENELEC Standards**: https://www.cenelec.eu/
- **IEEE Xplore**: https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/

## Glossary and Acronyms

### Common Terms
- **TSO**: Transmission System Operator
- **DSO**: Distribution System Operator
- **RFG**: Requirements for Generators
- **ENTSO-E**: European Network of TSOs for Electricity
- **LVRT/HVRT**: Low/High Voltage Ride Through
- **FSM**: Frequency Sensitive Mode
- **LFSM-O**: Limited FSM - Overfrequency
- **ROCOF**: Rate of Change of Frequency
- **PPM**: Power Park Module
- **PGPM**: Power Generating Module
- **PGM**: Power Generating Module
- **POC**: Point of Connection
- **FRT**: Fault Ride Through
- **SCADA**: Supervisory Control and Data Acquisition

### Full Glossary
Available in EU 2016/631 Article 2 (Definitions)
